# Literature Review

## Objective

The literature review investigates research related to:

- Diabetes nutrition
- Carbohydrate quality
- Dietary fibre
- Glycemic Index
- Meal planning
- Digital health applications
- Personalized nutrition

## Literature Review Format

For each research paper, record:

- Title
- Authors
- Publication year
- Research objective
- Study design
- Study population
- Main findings
- Limitations
- Relevance to DiaPlate
- DOI or source link

## Initial Reference

### American Diabetes Association: Standards of Care in Diabetes

**Organization:** American Diabetes Association

**Topic:** Diabetes care and nutrition recommendations

**Relevance:**

The standards discuss diabetes management and nutrition considerations.

They can help the team understand why meal recommendations should account for individual needs and why DiaPlate should remain an educational prototype.

**Source:**

https://professional.diabetes.org/standards-of-care

## Research Note

Research papers should be reviewed carefully.

The project should distinguish between:
- Findings directly supported by research
- Prototype design decisions
- Assumptions requiring further validation

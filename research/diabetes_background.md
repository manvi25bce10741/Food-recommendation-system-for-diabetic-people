# Diabetes Background

## Overview

Diabetes is a chronic health condition that occurs when blood sugar, also called blood glucose, is too high.

Type 1, Type 2, and gestational diabetes are different conditions that affect how the body processes blood glucose.

## Types of Diabetes

### Type 1 Diabetes

Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition in which the immune system attacks insulin-producing cells in the pancreas.

**Main characteristics:**
- The body produces little or no insulin.
- Insulin therapy is required.
- It can develop during childhood or adulthood.
- Blood glucose monitoring is important.

### Type 2 Diabetes

Type 2 diabetes occurs when the body does not use insulin effectively or does not produce enough insulin.

**Risk factors may include:**
- Family history
- Physical inactivity
- Excess body weight
- Age
- Other health-related factors

Management depends on the individual and may include lifestyle changes, medication, and monitoring.

### Gestational Diabetes

Gestational diabetes develops during pregnancy.

It requires appropriate medical monitoring because it can affect both the pregnant person and the baby.

## Relationship Between Food and Blood Glucose

Carbohydrates in food are broken down during digestion, producing glucose.

The effect of a meal on blood glucose depends on several factors, including:

- Amount of carbohydrate
- Type of carbohydrate
- Fibre content
- Portion size
- Food processing
- Physical activity
- Individual health conditions

## Importance of Dietary Planning

Dietary planning can help people organize meals and consider their nutritional needs.

Meal planning may support:
- Balanced food choices
- Consistent meal organization
- Awareness of carbohydrate intake
- Increased consumption of nutrient-dense foods

## Relevance to DiaPlate

DiaPlate aims to provide educational meal recommendations using user preferences and nutritional information.

The system is not intended to replace medical advice or individualized dietary planning by a qualified healthcare professional.
